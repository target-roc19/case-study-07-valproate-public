## Footnotes

[1] **Treatment Discontinuation Computation**: For each treatment (VPA, LEV), continuous episodes where the drug is being taken are identified from D3_UNIVARIATE_EPISODES. Episodes are concatenated until the first episode where the treatment indicator (IS_ON_VPA, IS_ON_LEV) becomes FALSE. The end_episode date of the last TRUE episode is recorded as the discontinuation date. If there are no episodes or all are FALSE, the date is set to NA.

[2] **Treatment Switch Logic**: Treatment switch represents the earliest date of switching to any alternative or additional antiepileptic medication. This includes:

- For exposed group: switching away from VPA (to LEV or OT_ASM)
- For control group: switching away from LEV (to VPA or OT_ASM)
- Both groups: initiating other antiepileptic drugs
- If the switch date occurs after conception date, it is set to NA as it is not relevant for the exposure window of interest

[3] **Treatment Discontinuation Split (Estimand-Specific)**: For estimand 2, treatment discontinuation is categorized relative to conception timing:

- `treatment_stop_more_than_3_months`: Treatment stopped >3 months before conception (father has washed out from exposure)
- `treatment_stop_3_months_or_less`: Treatment stopped ≤3 months before conception (father may still have exposure-related effects at conception)
- These variables are used for alternative censoring rules specific to each estimand

[4] **Database Disenrollment**: When individuals have multiple spells in D3_SPELLS (e.g., due to repeated registrations or re-entries), the earliest end_spell date that occurs after T0 is selected as the disenrollment date. This represents the first point where continuous observation ends in the database.

[5] **Estimand 1 Censoring**: Estimand 1 follows the intention-to-treat approach with censoring at:

- Conception (earliest_conception_date)
- Treatment switch to alternative antiepileptic (treatment_switch)
- Administrative censoring at 3 years (father_admin_censoring)
- No livebirth conception event (no_livebirth_conception, set to T0 + 3 years for those without observed livebirth)
- The reason for censoring indicates which event occurred first

[6] **Estimand 2 Censoring**: Estimand 2 represents a per-protocol or modified per-protocol analysis with extended censoring rules:

- Conception (earliest_conception_date)
- Treatment discontinuation >3 months before conception (treatment_stop_more_than_3_months) — more conservative exposure definition; creates earlier censoring for men who permanently stop treatment well before conception
- Treatment switch to alternative antiepileptic (treatment_switch)
- Administrative censoring at 3 years (father_admin_censoring)
- No livebirth conception event (no_livebirth_conception)
- The reason for censoring indicates which event occurred first

## Processing Steps

create_fup.R joins the study cohort to treatment episodes, conception and database-spell information. It derives treatment starts, switches, discontinuation and censoring dates, then calculates event indicators, follow-up durations, and person-years for both estimands.
