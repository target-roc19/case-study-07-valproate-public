## Footnotes

[1] **Database Disenrollment for Children**: Database disenrollment dates come from D3_SPELLS (the same spell table used for fathers). However, a child's disenrollment is determined independently of the father's disenrollment. When a child has multiple spells (e.g., re-registration or database re-entry), the earliest end_spell date occurring after T0 is selected. The child remains under observation during any gap periods and is only censored at the first spell termination.

[2] **Estimand 1 Censoring for Children**: Children are censored at the earliest of:
   - Any primary outcome event (diagnosis_autism, diagnosis_adhd, death_child, major_congenital_malformation)
   - Child reaches 8 years of age (age_8_years_old)
   - Database disenrollment (disenrolment_database_child)
   - Father's treatment switch (treatment_switch, censors child if father switches treatment during follow-up)
   - End of study period (end_study_period_child)
   - End of data collection (end_of_data_collection_child)
   - NOTE: If child is censored due to a father-level event (e.g., treatment_switch), the follow-up duration and person-years are set to NA to prevent negative follow-up times or spurious event rates

[3] **Father-Level Event Censoring**: When a child is censored due to a father-level event (treatment_switch or treatment_stop_more_than_3_months), the follow-up duration (fup_length_days) and person-years are explicitly set to NA. This prevents:
   - Negative follow-up times if the father event occurs before the child's birth
   - Spurious person-time accumulation for child outcomes when the father's exposure status changes
   - Misinterpretation of follow-up time in analyses

[4] **Outcome Observation Status**: A child's outcome is considered "observed" (outcome_observed = TRUE) if either:
   - An outcome event occurred (event = TRUE), OR
   - No event occurred AND the child reached 8 years of age (reached end of outcome ascertainment window)
   - This ensures that the absence of a recorded outcome in children who reached 8 years is treated as a true negative rather than missing data

[5] **Estimand 2 Censoring for Children**: Estimand 2 uses all estimand 1 censoring rules PLUS adds an alternative paternal exposure definition:
   - **treatment_stop_more_than_3_months** (instead of treatment_switch): Censors child when father permanently discontinues treatment >3 months before conception
   - This represents a per-protocol or modified per-protocol analysis where:
     - Fathers who wash out from treatment well before conception are followed for outcomes after exposure period ends
     - Fathers who briefly stop treatment (≤3 months before conception) are considered still exposed at conception
   - Creates potentially earlier censoring for children of fathers with early treatment discontinuation

## Additional Notes

- **Multiplets (Multiple Births per Father-T0)**: When a father has more than one live birth at the same T0 (e.g., twins, triplets), each child receives their own row in this table. Father-level variables (T0, group, treatment dates) are repeated for each child of the same father-T0 pair.

- **Missing Outcome Data Handling**: The `outcome_missing` indicator flags children with incomplete outcome ascertainment:
  - TRUE: Child censored before age 8 with no recorded outcome (data imputation will be applied)
  - FALSE: Child has complete outcome data (event observed or reached age 8 without event)

- **Outcome Definitions**: The four primary adverse outcomes in this study are:
  - **Major Congenital Malformation** (SV_MAJORCA): Structural birth defects affecting major organs/systems
  - **Autism Spectrum Disorder** (SV_AUTISM): Neurodevelopmental disorder diagnosed by clinical assessment or coded diagnosis
  - **ADHD** (SV_ADHD): Attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder diagnosed by clinical assessment or coded diagnosis
  - **Death** (SV_DEATH): Mortality during the follow-up period through age 8
  - **Composite Outcome**: Earliest date of any of the above four events

## Processing Steps

create_fup.R links children to father-trial records and anchored child outcomes. It derives outcome dates, child age limits, database disenrollment, paternal censoring, event status, follow-up duration, and person-years for both estimands.
