## Processing Steps

This transformations inputs the mother covariates ojects and male covariates objects. After restricting the male covariate objects to fathers, both set of covariates are merged based on father_id, T0 and trial_id - so that the final result has one row per children, with column representing the values of their mother/father covariates at eacth T0 in each trial.
