## Notes

Not on another antiepilectic drug had to be added: while not explicitly on the eligibility tables of the protocol, it was implicitly forbidden in the protocol (because no concurrent antiepilectic drugs are allowed). Follow-up ends at December 2024, but no individual will be enrolled with a starting data after March 2016.

## Example

Start enrollment is 2010-01-01, end_enrollment is 2016-03-01. Example:

Individual 1 is eligible at the first spell, because they are on valproate and fulfil all other eligible criteria. They also have a kid within 3 years and 9 from the spell send, so he is a father. He also has two kids, from 2 different pregnancies, with a conception date and date_of_birth after the spell start.

Individual 2 is not eligible at the first spell, because they are on valproate, but they are also on levetiracetam (e.g. they start valproate on that date, but they are still on levetiracetam). Note this is despite this individual and spell being in the eligible population (in_eligible_pop = TRUE). They also have no kids

Individual 3 is not eligible on the first spell, because they are on levetiracetam but are on another antiepilectic drug. But they become eligible in the second spell, when it is considered they are not anymore on another antiepilectic, but they are still continuing to take Levetiracetam.

Individual 4 is not eligible because even though they are valproate and no other drug, they are not fertile.

Individual 5 is elible in the first spell (are on valproate), and becomes ineligible at the second spell (when they start levetiracetam). They have twins within 3 years and 9 months of the end of the first spell, meaning they are an eligible father with respect to their first spell. \[This will later be flagged as treatment switch in a FUP object\]

Individual 6 is not eligibile because even though they are only on levetiracetam, they have a contraindication for valproate

Individual 7 is not eligibile because even though they are only on Valproate, they are not diagnosed with generalized epilepsy and therefore are not part of the eligible population.

## Processing Steps

This object determines if an individual is eligible at a a given time-point, consideirng is exposure status and status of variables that determine eligibility. Concretely, create_eligibility.R loads D3 multivariate episodes and D3 father-child links. It derives the overlap of a episode with the study-period, the exposure/control status, and computes the different eligibility flags. It also adds fatherhood information, used for creating the attrition tables, adding conception information after episodes.
